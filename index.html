Rules

The keeper system and the league constitution. Keeper rules are what you need before Aug 30; the constitution is the long version.

Keeper deadline: Friday, Aug 29 at 5:30 PM Mountain — 24 hours before the draft. Keeper selections may be changed up to that point and not after.

Keeper system

  1. A player can only be kept the following year if he is on your roster at the end of the season.
  2. When a player is drafted, his round value is locked for the year and cannot be altered. If he leaves your roster by drop or trade, he keeps that round value for the rest of the season.
  3. Keeping a player costs you that draft round. Draft him in the 4th this year and keep him, and you have no 3rd-round pick next year.
  4. Free agent pickups are assigned a round value from their projected half-PPR rank for the upcoming season, on the agreed list below.
  5. If a draft pick is traded away, the team that traded it forfeits its ability to keep anyone in that round. The other team must trade a pick back to balance the selections.
  6. No team may keep two players in the same round — keepers or franchise tags — even if it holds multiple picks in that round.
  7. A franchise tag locks a player at that round for the following draft, and he may then be kept for a maximum of one more year.
  8. A player traded during the off-season must be kept by the team that receives him.
  9. Teams may keep up to 5 players, or up to 4 players plus a franchise tag. If expansion teams are added that year, those numbers drop to 3, or 2 with a tag.
  10. Keeper selections may be changed up to 24 hours before the draft start time.

10 teams, 16 rounds. Rosters hold 16 players at all times during the regular season, plus 2 IR spots that do not count against the 16. Any empty spot must be filled within one week. Maximum 3 K and 3 D/ST. A player may not sit in your active lineup more than one week after an IR designation.

Scoring — ESPN half PPR

ESPN's standard half-PPR scoring. Half a point per reception; one point per 25 passing yards and per 10 rushing or receiving yards.

Trades, waivers and the deadline

Unlimited trades and add/drops per season. Every proposed trade sits in a two-day review, during which owners vote to allow or reject it. If 50% of owners reject, the commissioner nullifies the trade.

The commissioner may veto a trade that contravenes the constitution or is contrary to the best interests of fantasy football. A veto must be announced within 24 hours and is reviewable.

Trade deadline: Wednesday at 12pm during week 7. No trades in week 7.

Draft picks and "players to be named later" are non-transferable and may not form part of a trade.

Free agents come through the weekly waiver wire, in reverse standings order — last place claims first. Each free agent sits on waivers for two days. No season acquisition limit.

Playoffs and payouts

Playoffs begin week 14. Three rounds, one week each.

The six clubs with the highest win percentage qualify. The top two get a first-round bye.

Ties are broken first by head-to-head, then by total points for in the regular season.

Champion $600 · Runner-up $300 · 3rd place $100. Paid within one week of the championship.

Buy-in is set by vote each off-season and handled by the commissioner via Venmo. Miss the buy-in date and your club is locked out of its ESPN controls, all drafting privileges and all players are benched — which counts as Inactivity.

The commissioner, and how to overrule him

The commissioner is the chief administrative, dispute-resolution and financial officer of the league. He reviews keeper selections and transactions, rules on interpretations, and may investigate any act alleged not to be in the best interests of fantasy football.

Penalties available to him: a formal warning · a reprimand · reversing or denying a transaction · forfeiting a period of competition · forfeiting a draft pick · reducing waiver priority · suspension or removal · forfeiting annual dues. Any decision carrying a penalty must be posted formally.

Any decision of the commissioner can be overturned. The process:

  1. Clubs with a direct interest in the outcome are Interested Parties and cannot vote.
  2. One third of eligible clubs must petition for reconsideration, within one week of the decision being posted. Without a petition there is no review.
  3. If the commissioner declines to reconsider, a vote is held.
  4. A two-thirds majority of eligible clubs vacates the decision. Anything less and it stands.

The commissioner serves a minimum term of three years, has no term limit, and is elected by simple majority. No election may be held within 60 days of the draft.

Voting

Off-season votes cover the election of a commissioner, the buy-in, the payout schedule, and amendments to the constitution — including expansion clubs, league settings and draft regulations.

Most votes are a simple majority. Constitutional amendments need two thirds. Amending during the regular season needs a unanimous vote.

All votes stay open for 3 days. There is no minimum quorum.

Roster management and inactivity

Managers must field a complete team at all times.

A player on Injured Reserve may not stay in your active lineup for more than one week after his IR designation.

Inactive means neglecting both to add or drop players and to roster a full starting lineup. Being inactive for more than two weeks is an offence, and is grounds for permanent suspension.

Offences

Conduct beyond a minor contravention. Everyone commits an offence who engages in:

Bribery — providing value other than in-game value in exchange for in-game value.

Collusion — agreeing with another party to limit open competition. Assumed in any case where one manager acts for more than one club.

Disrespect — comments a reasonable person would consider in excess of friendly trash talk.

Harassment — repeatedly contacting someone after they have asked you not to. It is a defence that the contact was objectively reasonable in tone, content and frequency.

Non-competitive behaviour — demonstrating an intention not to compete, beyond mere negligence.

Nuisance — complaining about the league to the point of harassing the commissioner or markedly decreasing everyone else's enjoyment.

General Anti-Avoidance Provision — conduct not otherwise prohibited but generally acknowledged to be shitty.

Penalties run up to permanent suspension without refund. The commissioner may appoint a neutral arbitrator or an advisory jury, and must do so if he is himself an interested party. Jury votes are advisory only and may not be secret.
